First release: routes the shell commands of Pi's bash tool and of exec_command tools (pi-unified-exec and compatible plugins, codemode calls included) through rtk rewrite for compact output, with configurable adapters for other exec tools, enforcement of RTK denies, skip rules for PTY sessions and non-POSIX shells, fail-open handling with suspension of a failing rtk, and a /rtk command.
